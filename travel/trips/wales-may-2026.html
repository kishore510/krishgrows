Pistyll Rhaeadr
📍 Open in Maps
Tallest waterfall in England or Wales — 80m single drop. Car park right next to it, café on site. Just 2 minutes flat walk to the base. On your route via A490 from Oswestry.
♿ 2-min flat walk£5 parking · 50p WC⭐ 4.7

🚂 Steam Railway
Afternoon · 2 hrs
Bala Lake Railway
📍 Open in Maps
Narrow gauge steam train along the shores of Llyn Tegid — Wales’s largest natural lake. No walking needed. Start from Llanuwchllyn station. Book ahead at bala-lake-railway.co.uk.
♿ Sit back, no walking~£18 adult return⭐ 4.6

Swallow Falls

5 minutes from base. Paved viewing platforms with handrails — no rough terrain. Very powerful falls, completely accessible for all ages. £2 entry, card accepted.

💧 Waterfall
Mid-morning · 1.5 hrs
Aber Falls (Rhaeadr Fawr)
📍 Open in Maps
Highest rated attraction in North Wales. 1.4-mile flat path — Lord of the Rings scenery — to a stunning 37m waterfall. Wheelchair friendly. Arrive before 10am for parking.
♿ Flat pathFree · £5 parking⭐ 4.8

Practical Notes

Before You Go

🚂
Book Bala Railway First

Sells out in advance. Book at bala-lake-railway.co.uk. Start from Llanuwchllyn for free parking.

🛣️
Pistyll Rhaeadr Road

Last 3 miles are narrow single-track. Take it steady — allow extra time.

🕘
Aber Falls Early Start

Car park fills fast in summer. Arrive before 10am. Free entry, £5 parking.

💷
Pistyll Rhaeadr Cash

Bring coins — £5 parking, 50p toilets. No card machine on site.

🏛️
National Trust Tip

Penrhyn Castle free with NT membership — saves ~£30 for two people.

🍴
Evening Dinner

Pont y Pair Inn, Betws-y-Coed — 5 min drive, 4.2★, great pub food, dog friendly.
