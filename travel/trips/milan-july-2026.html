⇄ Alternative to Day 4
Lugano Day Trip
Milano Centrale → Lugano → Milano Centrale → Malpensa (MXP) · flight 22:30 · forecast ~27°C, ~80% chance of storms
Swaps Bergamo for a Swiss lake town — same flight, same buffer, different Sunday. With an 80% chance of afternoon thunderstorms forecast for the 26th, this plan front-loads the viewpoint to the morning and keeps the afternoon flexible (lake cruise if clear, covered arcades if not). Lugano also runs noticeably quieter than Lake Como in peak season — Como pulls ~1.4 million visitors a year with boats needing advance booking; Lugano's boats are typically buy-on-the-day. A passport is needed for the Swiss border (quick/light for UK passport holders), and Switzerland uses Swiss Francs, not Euros.

Practical Notes

Before You Go

🎟️
Book the Duomo Lift Slot

The 11am rooftop entry is pre-booked — a reserved-ticket queue (~15-20 min) beats the multi-hour walk-up line. Museum, Archaeological Area and Crypt are optional add-ons, skipped here by choice.

🍮
IT Milano — Brera's Only Visit

Michelin-starred tiramisu, but this is the only time the schedule is in Brera — prioritise it over extra wandering if short on time.

🚕
Taxi & Uber Black Backup

Licensed private drivers are unaffected by public transport strikes — keep as a backup for either day.

📱
Compare FreeNow & Uber

Both worth having installed to compare pricing and availability if ATM does strike.

🚶
Friday Is One Flowing Walk

Duomo → Castello → Park → Brera, deliberately routed so no leg fully depends on public transport.

💶
Carry Small Change

Panzerotti, church donations and short taxi hops are easier with coins and small notes on hand.

🌐
Check atm.it

A few days before, for a definitive answer on whether Milan's own metro/tram network is affected.

🎫
Como Villa Tickets

The combined Villa Monastero + Cipressi ticket must be bought in person — it can't be pre-booked online.

⛴️
Book the Hydrofoil Ahead

Bellagio → Como hydrofoil tickets can't be bought onboard. Buy online or at the terminal — it runs only 4-6 times daily.

🕰️
Check Trenord & Navigazione Laghi

A few days before either day trip, for any last-minute train or ferry disruption.

🧳
Bergamo Needs No Booking

Every site on the Bergamo day is walk-up — no advance tickets required anywhere.

🇨🇭
Lugano Alt — Passport & Francs

Switzerland isn't an EU/Schengen internal hop — bring a passport (checks are usually quick for UK holders). Carry some CHF or a no-foreign-fee card; Euros are often accepted but at a poor rate.

🚡
Monte Brè — the Other Viewpoint

If San Salvatore doesn't appeal, Monte Brè is the alternative funicular — a similar though slightly less panoramic view, with the option to walk down to Brè village after.
