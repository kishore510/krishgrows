What's in the Ground

South Asian vegetables grown from seed in North Yorkshire. Updated through the season.

🌿
Bed 1
Karela

Bitter gourd grown from South Asian seed stock. Needs warm soil and a climbing frame — going in the ground after last frost.

🥒
Bed 2
Tinda

Indian round gourd — compact plant, prolific fruiter. Started under the Spider Farmer shelf light in late March.

🍅
Bed 3
Tomatoes

Mix of varieties. Hardened off and planted out. Spider Farmer shelf gave them a strong start — thick stems, deep green.

🌶️
Bed 4
Chillies & Peppers

Multiple varieties including Indian finger chillies. Still under grow lights — chillies need a long season in Yorkshire.
